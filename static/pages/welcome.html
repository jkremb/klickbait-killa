What gets shared

Each time you click Summarize, the video's title, channel, duration and transcript are sent to using your key. The addon doesn't run any servers and has no analytics.

The addon gets transcripts from YouTube through your normal browser session, including your YouTube login if you have one. It does this only after you click Summarize.

Summaries are stored unencrypted in Firefox on this device, so the list of summarized videos could be seen by someone with access to your Firefox profile. You can clear them anytime in settings.

Protect your key

Your API key is encrypted with this passphrase. You'll enter it once per Firefox session, the first time a summary needs it.

It can't be recovered. If you forget it, the only option is a full reset, which deletes your keys, settings and saved summaries.

You're set

On YouTube, hover over a thumbnail or open a video and click Summarize.
